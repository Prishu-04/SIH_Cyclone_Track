DMCA & Copyright

How to report copyright infringement, and what we allow through our file-upload fields.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. What can be uploaded on this Site

Two forms accept a file: the emergency help-request form and the missing-person report form. Both are restricted to a single optional photo, enforced both in your browser and again on the server:

  • Formats: JPEG, PNG, or WEBP only. SVG and other formats are rejected — an SVG can embed executable script, so we don't accept it even though it's technically an "image."
  • Size: 2MB maximum.
  • Purpose: a photo relevant to the emergency or missing-person report only — not a general file-sharing feature.

There is no public gallery, feed, or search of uploaded photos — they're stored for authorised responders only (see Responder Access), which meaningfully limits (but doesn't eliminate) copyright and illegal-content exposure compared to a public posting feature.

2. By uploading, you confirm

  • You own the photo, or have the right to submit it for this purpose.
  • It doesn't infringe anyone else's copyright, trademark, or other rights.
  • It's relevant to the report you're filing, and doesn't contain illegal content.

3. Reporting a copyright claim (DMCA-style takedown)

If you believe content on this Site infringes your copyright, email dmca@cyclotrackindia.example with:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  • The specific location of the material on this Site (a reference ID, page URL, or description).
  • Your contact information (name, address, phone, email).
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the above is accurate and that you're authorised to act on the copyright owner's behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

We'll review valid notices and remove or disable access to the identified material. (Placeholder contact — the team should replace this with a real monitored address, and consult a lawyer before relying on this as a formal DMCA safe-harbor policy.)

4. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, contact the same address with the material's location, your contact details, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed in error.

5. Reporting other illegal or abusive content

For anything else — illegal content that isn't a copyright claim — use the same contact address, or call 112 if it describes an ongoing emergency.

6. Related technical safeguards

Beyond file-type/size restrictions, the backend rate-limits form submissions and the AI chat endpoint per visitor to reduce automated abuse. See Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the fuller picture.

Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use CycloTrack India, why, who can see it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Applies to cyclotrackindia (the "Site"), operated by Team Nexus for Smart India Hackathon 2026.

Prototype notice. CycloTrack India is a Smart India Hackathon prototype, not a registered company. This policy describes, honestly, what the software actually does with your data today. It is a starting point, not a substitute for legal advice — before any real-world deployment handling real emergency data, have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction.

1. What we collect

Emergency help requests (report.html)

Full name, mobile number, email (optional), state, district, village/city, current location text, an optional GPS latitude/longitude, number of people/children/elderly affected, emergency type(s), a free-text description, and an optional photo.

Missing-person reports (missing-person.html)

The missing person's name, age, gender, last-seen date/time and location, identifying details, an optional photo, and the reporting contact's name and phone number.

CycloBot AI assistant

Messages you type into the chat widget are sent to OpenRouter, a third-party AI provider, to generate a reply. We don't ask for personal details in chat and you shouldn't share any — don't include your name, phone number, or exact location in a chat message.

Device storage (no account needed)

We use your browser's own storage — never sent to us or anyone else — for small conveniences: remembering you've seen the intro animation, your chosen chatbot language, and, only if our server is unreachable when you submit a form, a local copy of that submission so it isn't lost.

Map tiles

The live map loads imagery from Esri, OpenStreetMap/OpenTopoMap contributors, and a public AWS elevation-tile dataset. Loading a map tile sends your IP address to those providers, the same as loading any image from a website.

2. Why we collect it

  • To route your emergency or missing-person report to people who can act on it.
  • To let CycloBot generate a real, relevant answer to your question.
  • To keep the site itself working (map rendering, offline fallback, language preference).

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Who can see it

Submitted help requests and missing-person reports are not public. They are stored on the server and can only be read back through a token-gated "responder" endpoint, intended for authorised disaster-response personnel. See Responder Access. If our server can't be reached when you submit, your data is instead kept only in your own browser until it can sync — nobody but you can see it in that state.

4. How long we keep it

Submissions are appended to a server-side log and kept until a team member deletes them — this prototype does not yet have an automatic retention/deletion schedule. If you want your submission deleted, contact us (below) with your reference ID (e.g. HR-2026... or MP-2026...) and we will remove that record.

5. Your choices

  • Access / deletion: email us your reference ID and we'll locate, share, or delete that record.
  • Local data: clearing your browser's site data removes anything saved on your device (intro flag, language, offline queue).
  • Location: GPS capture on the report form is opt-in — you choose whether to share it.

6. Children's data

This site is a disaster-response tool, not a service aimed at children. If a form is submitted on behalf of a child (e.g. a missing-person report), only the minimum details needed to help find or assist them should be included.

7. Security

Submissions are validated and rate-limited before being stored. Personal data is never exposed through a public API — only aggregate counts are public. That said, no system is perfectly secure; don't include anything in a submission beyond what's needed for a responder to help.

8. Contact

Questions, access requests, or deletion requests: privacy@cyclotrackindia.example (placeholder — the team should replace this with a real monitored address before this policy is relied upon).

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above when this policy changes materially.

Terms of Service

The rules for using CycloTrack India, and — most importantly — the limits of what it can promise you.

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Applies to CycloTrack India (the "Site"), a Smart India Hackathon 2026 prototype by Team Nexus.

This is not an emergency service. In a life-threatening situation, call 112 (or your local emergency number) directly. Do not wait for a response through this Site.

1. Acceptance of terms

By using CycloTrack India, you agree to these Terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the Site. We may update these Terms as the project develops; continued use after a change means you accept the update.

2. What this Site is

CycloTrack India is a research and decision-support prototype built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem SIH26070, Ministry of Earth Sciences). It provides cyclone tracking visualisations, a historical archive, an AI assistant, and tools for submitting emergency help requests and missing-person reports.

3. No warranty — predictions and demo data

  • Cyclone tracks, intensity forecasts, landfall predictions, storm-surge and flood estimates, and risk scores shown on this Site are demo/prototype outputs, not official forecasts, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
  • Shelters, alerts, and data-source status shown in the Response Center are demo fixtures for this prototype, not a live, verified registry.
  • The Site is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including accuracy, availability, or fitness for a particular purpose.
  • Always follow official IMD (mausam.imd.gov.in) and NDMA advisories and local-authority instructions over anything shown here.

4. Emergency and missing-person submissions

  • Submitting a help request or missing-person report does not guarantee a response time, or any response at all — this prototype does not currently operate a staffed response team.
  • You confirm that information you submit is accurate to the best of your knowledge, and that you have the right to submit any photo you upload.
  • See our Privacy Policy for how this data is stored and who can access it.

5. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • Submit false emergency or missing-person reports, or knowingly false information in any form.
  • Upload content you don't have the rights to, or that is illegal, harassing, or infringes someone else's rights (see our DMCA / Copyright Policy).
  • Attempt to overwhelm, scrape at abusive volume, or disrupt the Site's services (see rate limits noted in our technical policies).
  • Attempt to access the responder-only endpoints without authorisation.

6. The AI assistant (CycloBot)

CycloBot is an AI system and can be wrong. It does not have live/real-time storm data. Treat its answers as educational, not as an authoritative or emergency source — the assistant itself is instructed to say so and point you to IMD/NDMA for live bulletins.
